Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Dudley Email Triage is a personal tool used only by its owner, Steven Roesch, on his own Gmail account.

What it accesses

It reads message headers, subjects, a short excerpt of message text, and attachment file names in the owner's inbox, and it adds or removes labels on those messages. It does not send, draft, or delete email.

How data is handled

All processing happens on the owner's own computer. Email data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Access credentials are stored locally and readable only by the owner.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
